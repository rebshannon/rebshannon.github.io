My research interests lie in using computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to solve multiscale, multiphase problems. Currently, I am working to model shock-droplet interactions with a focus on the role of cavitation in the droplet breakup process.

I have over 5 years of experience using [OpenFOAM][3] to solve multiphase problems, including implementing new equations of state and modifying the solver as necessary. I also have experience using the CFD programs [Mulitcomponent Flow Code][1] and [ECOGEN][2].
