## Fleet Feet Longmeadow

### Outfitter

From May 2021 - May 2022, I worked part time at Fleet Feet Longmeadow, a local run specialty store. In this position, I provided personalized assistance to customers looking for new shoes, insoles, and other running gear. I also lead weekly fun runs, and helped organize a 5k/10k training program.

## Holyoke Gas and Electric

### Cadet Engineer, Hydroelectric

During my undergraduate degree, I spent my summer and winter breaks working in the Hydroelectric Division of the Holyoke Gas and Electric Department. I had a wide range of assignments during my time here, so I had to be ready for anything. A few highlights include
<ul> 
    <li>safety inspections of fencing and signage along the 4.5 mile-long canal system</li>
    <li>initiation of flood response drills</li>
    <li>prepared a building condition assessment, including reccomendations for what maintenance should be prioritized</li>
    <li>tagging and cataloguing of all valves in the system</li>
    <li>observed construction and upgrades being performed on the dam, fish passage facilities, and generating units</li>
    <li>analyzed changed in generator efficiency after maintenance</li>
    <li>helped with fishway operations including counting fish and transporting American eels</li>
</ul>

## Western New England University - Student Accessibility Services

### Volunteer Notetaker

For several of my undergraduate courses, I volunteered with Student Accessibility Services. After each lecture, I sent copies of my course notes to the office, and they shared them with students who were unable to take notes on their own. I held this position in four courses: Computer Programming for Engineers, Differential Equations, Calculus III, and Engineering Analysis.

## Holyoke Boys and Girls Club

In Spring 2016 and 2017, I volunteered at my local Boys and Girls Club at their after school program. I helped elementary school aged children comlete their homework assignments and supervised free play time. During Summer 2016, I worked part time in their summer program as a counsellor.
