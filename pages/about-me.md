I earned my Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Western New England University (WNE), graduating Summa Cum Laude in 2021. I was awarded the Dean's Award for maintaining the highest cumulative GPA in the School of Engineering and was named a co-recipiet of the Marston Award, which is given to the senior students judged to have the most mature understanding of the principles of professional engineering practice. I was also inducted into the Tau Beta Pi Honor Society (an engineering honor society) in my junior year and was elected treasurer. 

I spent my summer and winter breaks as a Cadet Engineer at the Holyoke Gas and Electric Department. I worked in the hydroelectric department, which maintains and operates hydroeletric turbines that generate about two thirds of the city's electricity demands. In this role, I helped with everything from leading safety inspections to evaluating generator efficiency after maintenance to transporting juvenile American eels around the dam as they migrate upstream. During the academic year, I also worked as a teaching assistant for first year engineering design courses and a grader for higher level engineering courses.
{:.clear-wrap}

After completing my undergaduate work, I stayed at WNE and earned my Masters of Science in Mechanical Engineering in 2022. I continued to work as a grader and teaching assistant, and I also worked part time at Fleet Feet Longmeadow. I was named a Student Facilitator by the Northeast Cyberteam, which gave me resources to complete computational research with the Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center. This allowed me to complete my [Master's Thesis][1], which focused on computational fluid dynamcis and machine learning.

In 2022, I began my PhD at Boston University (BU) working with Dr. Sheryl Grace in the Unsteady Fluid Mechanics and Acoustics Lab. I was awarded the College of Engineering Distringuished Fellowship in Energy and Sustainability based on my previous experience and application materials. In 2024, I was awarded a National Defense in Science and Engineering Graduate Fellowship sponsored by the Office of Naval Research. 

Outside of wor, I was elected Communications Chair of the Student Association of Graduate Engineers at BU. In this role, I helped organize events for my peers, maintained an Instagram account for the organization, and wrote monthly newsletters. I'm also an avid runner and recently started to explore the triathlon world. I love playing basketball and doing almost anything outside, but also enjoy staying inside and quilting or reading (mostly sci-fi).
